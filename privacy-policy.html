Privacy Notice.

How Team PSR handles personal information in connection with enquiries, surveys, research and related services.

Effective date: 25 November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Team PSR handles personal information in connection with its website, enquiries, surveys, market-research activities and related data services. It should be read together with any project-specific notice provided to participants.

1. Who we are

“Team PSR” is the business name used for market-research, survey and data-service activities. Team PSR is not a separately incorporated company or registered legal entity. This website and the services described on it are operated under the Team PSR name. For privacy questions or requests, contact Team PSR at sales@teampsr.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to personal information handled through this website and in connection with Team PSR’s enquiries, market-research, survey and related project activities, to the extent Team PSR determines how that information is handled. A client may separately determine the purposes and means of processing for a particular project. Where a project-specific privacy notice or client instruction applies, that information should also be read.

3. Information we may collect or receive

Depending on the website interaction or project, information may include:

  • contact details such as name, email address and telephone number;
  • survey answers, opinions, feedback and other responses;
  • telephone or other research-call recordings and associated voice data;
  • project-related information such as language, broad region or survey metadata, where collected;
  • messages, enquiries and correspondence; and
  • basic technical information generated when using the website, such as browser or device information, where made available by hosting or website services.

We aim to handle information relevant to the enquiry or project and do not ask people to provide sensitive information through the general website contact form.

4. How information is collected

Information may be collected directly from a person through a website enquiry, telephone survey, interview or other research activity; received from a client that commissioned a project; or generated in the course of delivering and administering a project. In the call-centre and survey operations described by Team PSR, participants have been verbally informed that calls are recorded for research purposes and that their data would be kept safe and they could request deletion. The exact notice and collection process may vary by project. Team PSR does not represent that this general description proves consent to every possible later use.

5. Purposes for which information is used

Information may be used, as relevant to the project, to:

  • respond to enquiries and communicate with clients, participants and project contacts;
  • conduct and administer surveys, interviews and market-research activities;
  • review, analyse and summarise research responses and prepare project deliverables;
  • manage quality, operational processes, security and project records;
  • perform agreed services and meet applicable contractual or legal obligations; and
  • handle complaints, requests, disputes and other legitimate business administration.

6. Disclosure and sharing

Information may be shared with the client that commissioned the relevant project and with service providers or project partners where sharing is necessary for the project, authorised and permitted by applicable requirements. We do not make personal information public as a general practice.

Research-call recording information or a general statement that a call is recorded for research does not, by itself, establish permission for every subsequent disclosure or commercial use. Before supplying recordings or datasets for AI model training or evaluation, commercial licensing, resale or onward sublicensing, the relevant client rights, participant permissions and other applicable requirements must be assessed for that dataset and proposed use. Team PSR will not describe such permissions as obtained unless they are supported by the relevant records or authorisations.

7. Storage, security and retention

Team PSR uses reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Access should be limited to people who need the information for the relevant enquiry or project. Information may be stored using systems operated by Team PSR, its clients or service providers, depending on the project.

There is no single retention period for every type of information or project. Retention depends on the project instructions, the purpose for which the information was collected, applicable contractual or legal requirements and the need to address requests or disputes. Information should not be retained longer than necessary for the relevant purpose and applicable obligations.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, a person may contact Team PSR to ask about personal information relating to them, request access or correction, request deletion, raise a concern or withdraw a permission where withdrawal is available. Some requests may require reasonable verification. A request may be subject to legal, contractual or project-related requirements; if Team PSR cannot fully comply, it will explain the reason where appropriate.

To make a privacy request, email sales@teampsr.com with the subject line “Privacy request”. Please identify the relevant survey or project if known. Do not email sensitive identity documents or another person’s personal information unless Team PSR has explained why it is needed and provided an appropriate way to submit it.

9. Children and additional safeguards

The general website and enquiry process is not intended to collect personal information from children. If a particular research project may involve children or other persons requiring additional safeguards, that project must be separately assessed and appropriate notices, permissions and safeguards must be established before collection.

10. Website hosting, forms and external links

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The contact form may use a third-party form-processing service to deliver enquiries to Team PSR. Those providers may process technical or submission information under their own terms and privacy notices. This website may also contain links to third-party websites; Team PSR is not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services. Please avoid submitting confidential or sensitive information through the general contact form.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Team PSR’s website, services, information-handling practices or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.
